The lifecycle

Five stages.
Zero ambiguity.

Scroll through a real project. Watch the ledger record every move on the right, exactly as SwitchKey writes it.

Stage 01 / Lock

Baseline locked

The creator defines deliverables, roles, acceptance rules, and payment amounts, then locks baseline v1.0. From here, the terms are binding.

Stage 02 / Accept

Roles accepted

Each vendor reviews the locked terms and accepts their role. Acceptance is written to the ledger with their identity and a timestamp.

Stage 03 / Deliver

Evidence submitted

Work is delivered as evidence against the criteria. Files, links, and notes attach to the exact deliverable they satisfy.

Stage 04 / Approve

Stage approved

The creator reviews the evidence and approves. That single action is the payment instruction. There is nothing extra to file.

Stage 05 / Settle

Settled through Stripe

Funds move from Settling to Settled automatically. The vendor is paid, the record is closed, and the export is ready.
